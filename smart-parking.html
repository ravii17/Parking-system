What We Offer

Packed with intelligence.

Every part of ParkSmart is built to make parking seamless — from entry to exit.

🔍

Real-Time Sensor Grid

IoT ultrasonic sensors in every bay transmit live occupancy data every 5 seconds, ensuring accuracy you can trust.

📱

Mobile Reservations

Book your spot up to 48 hours in advance from any device. Receive an instant QR code for seamless gate entry.

🚧

Smart Entry Gates

ANPR (automatic number plate recognition) cameras open gates automatically when your vehicle is detected.

💡

LED Guidance System

Green/red LED indicators above each bay guide drivers directly to the nearest available spot instantly.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Owners get a full admin portal with occupancy heatmaps, peak-hour trends, and revenue reports.

🔔

Alerts & Overstay Detection

Automatic SMS alerts for reservation expiry and overstay penalties. Never let a spot be blocked for too long.
